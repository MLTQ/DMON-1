# `dmon-organism-research.tex`

## Purpose

Provides a self-contained working research paper on DMON-1 and SOL2. It explains the
organism concept, architecture, methodological discipline, completed results, calibrated
limitations, and near-term research agenda for readers who have not followed the repo's
experiment-by-experiment history.

## Components

### Front matter and evidence vocabulary

- Introduces the persistent neural-organism goal and detachable-language-organ framing.
- Separates established contracts, mechanistic evidence, diagnostics, and open claims.

### Architecture

- Documents typed tissue, sparse directed dendrites, bounded cellular dynamics, private
  cell expression/adapters, stream memory, dedicated output sinks, and append-only growth.
- Includes equations and original TikZ diagrams derived from repository contracts.

### Experimental synthesis

- Summarizes SOL, Grok, and Fable lessons without inheriting over-strong single-test
  conclusions.
- Reports SOL2 S0-R, S1-P through S1-P4, L0, and L0-C1 results with their original
  attribution boundaries.

### Agenda and references

- Separates demonstrated achievements from simultaneous retention, useful growth,
  language memory, continual learning, and metabolism goals.
- Cites primary literature on NCA, transformers, NTM/DNC memory, liquid networks,
  e-prop, and Llama 3.

## Contracts

| Dependent | Expects | Breaking changes |
|---|---|---|

## Notes

- The source is primary; the compiled PDF is written to
  `output/pdf/dmon-organism-research.pdf`.
- The paper reports the L0-C1g 25-update run as a diagnostic and explicitly identifies
  the 300-update continuation as post-hoc exploratory work.
- Future result additions should update both the relevant experiment protocol and this
  synthesis, then recompile and visually inspect every page.
