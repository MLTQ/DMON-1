# SOL2 program review: what the experiments actually support

Status: synthesis after S1-P through S1-P4 and L0-C1 through C1z, before C1aa.

## Intended object

DMON is the persistent organism, not a wrapper that retrieves text for an LLM. Its
cellular state, differentiated anatomy, plasticity, development, and lifetime history
must eventually determine behavior. A frozen or replaceable language model is a
Broca-like organ: it supplies learned language perception and production, while DMON
owns memory, mode, intent, adaptation, organ coordination, and continuity.

The near-term wiki task is therefore an operation-learning assay: can a passage alter
the living substrate, survive context erasure, and later change language behavior for
the right reason? It is not the final training distribution and fact recall is not the
project's definition of intelligence.

## Signals that support the substrate

1. S1-P and S1-P2 show that SOL2 can master a deep generated procedure. Internal,
   compute, relay, and topology lesions cost roughly 47--86 accuracy points, so organs
   are not doing all the work. A mastered organism learned a new physical organ about
   1,000 updates faster than scratch and recovered a detached organ rapidly.
2. Private transition adapters became jointly causal in S1-P3b. This directly repairs
   the old shared-transition limitation: cells share a genome but can express private
   local computation. Some unused cells remain legitimate reserve.
3. Cumulative anchors in S1-P4 moved the simultaneous A/B frontier from about 14/93 to
   64/60. Pressure-triggered growth appended neurons without corrupting the creature.
   The grown cells were not recruited, but the retention pressure and structural
   operation were mechanically real.
4. At the language boundary, a frozen Qwen 3.5 4B teacher solves the compact bindings,
   accepts finite prefix gradients, and can be behaviorally steered by organism output.
   Memory state carries incompatible-passage differences; coherent addressing learns;
   every recurrent subsystem has received causal gradient in the best treatments.
5. C1z is the first local treatment to improve correct passage against wrong passage
   in both development and held-out mean loss. The direction of its paired signal is
   therefore supported even though the effect is too small and inconsistent.

## Signals that reject simple continuation

1. Larger organisms and longer runs have already been isolated. More time sharpened
   addressing while value separation and control collapsed; fourfold larger tissue did
   not repair the route. Capacity is not the measured bottleneck.
2. Absolute targets repeatedly learn common modes. C1y achieved excellent recall and
   relay alignment while retaining only about one tenth of the counterfactual answer
   difference. Local alignment alone cannot establish memory use.
3. The single pre-transformer prefix is functional but poorly conditioned. The
   organism often learns a static intervention, a harmful passage effect, or silence.
   A successful internal route may still require a richer, differentiated language
   organ at several transformer depths.
4. Continuous meta-training lanes saturated their finite FIFO and recurrent state.
   Fresh episodes repaired gradient retention but not semantics. Fresh episodes are a
   developmental population used to learn a genome; they are not evidence for the
   intended one-creature continuous lifetime.
5. Protecting mature tissue creates a stability/plasticity frontier but does not make
   reserve or newly grown cells attractive. Growth must follow a usable local learning
   route and a demand signal that is cheapest to satisfy through new tissue.
6. Four-choice wiki accuracy is a plumbing metric, not the project objective. It must
   always be interpreted beside correct-vs-wrong passage likelihood, reset and lesion
   controls, internal utilization, and later continuous-lifetime retention.

## Verdict

The cellular-organism research direction remains feasible and is better supported by
the procedural experiments than the recent wiki failures alone suggest. The specific
strategy of scaling the creature, extending training, or adding progressively larger
absolute auxiliary losses is not supported.

C1aa is justified as one bounded developmental-scaffolding test because it changes the
one factor C1z identified: the exact paired teaching direction had variable weak scale
and competed with an easier external effect objective. It is not a claim that neurons
should preserve one coordinate forever, nor a final lifetime training regime.

After C1aa the decision boundary is strict:

- If the normalized staged treatment cannot preserve the paired delta locally, stop
  this exact coherent-copy route. Do not respond with more cells or more updates.
- If it preserves the route but cannot control Qwen causally, promote a differentiated
  multi-depth language organ: several bounded low-rank effectors read only output
  tissue and inject at selected frozen transformer depths. Train paired behavior and
  lesion attribution; treat same-coordinate transport only as temporary scaffolding.
- If it passes behavior, remove the scaffold gradually, restore interleaved continuous
  lifetimes, then combine anchors, plasticity pressure, and growth. Only recruited new
  tissue counts as development.

The next major curriculum should move from isolated fact bindings to persistent modes
and patterns of thought: interleaved procedural contexts, delayed return to earlier
modes, novel organ attachment, and language-mediated plans whose success cannot be
supplied by the frozen language model alone.

## C1aa confirmation

C1aa subsequently held the detached target at exact RMS `0.05` and removed competing
language rewards for 25 updates. Recall retention remained about 6% and declined in the
tail; correct passage still lost to wrong passage held out. Stage B was therefore
stopped by rule. This confirms the review's boundary: the project should now leave the
exact same-coordinate single-recall route rather than answer with more capacity,
duration, or loss weight.
