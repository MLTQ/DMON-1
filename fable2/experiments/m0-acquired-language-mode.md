# M0: acquired response-language mode through the Broca graft

Status: preregistered 2026-08-07, before any mode-corpus generation or optimizer
update. First experiment of the M-line, following the operator decision to defer
G3 and pivot to the substrate's demonstrated competence.

## Question

Can the organism acquire a **mode** — a persistent, low-dimensional behavioral
policy, here the response language — from demonstrations experienced only
through its own tissue, carry it across context erasure, and express it by
shifting frozen Qwen's continuation likelihoods on prompts it has never seen?

## Why modes, why now

Three consecutive experiments (C1 series, G1, G2) falsified exact-content
transport through tissue activations, individually exonerating capacity,
duration, and data. The same experiments repeatedly demonstrated the
complementary capability: coarse, global, low-dimensional *modulation* of the
frozen model works (steering diagnostics, generic transport, mode-like control).
Response language is the cleanest such axis: binary, token-measurable,
content-controllable by translation pairing, and native to Qwen. M0 asks the
organism to do what the evidence says it can, under the full causal-assay
discipline. Deliberately absent: arbitrary-symbol conventions (episodic binding
in disguise — H-line/M2 territory).

## Task

- **Mode pair**: French (A) vs German (B) responses.
- **Exposure**: K=4 demonstration exchanges (English prompt + response in the
  exposed mode, each ≤ 56 tokens so the full stream fits the 256-slot memory
  FIFO) streamed through the organism as frozen features, memory writes on. The
  scoring context never contains the demonstrations, any instruction, or any
  French/German text.
- **Scoring**: a neutral English prompt with two candidate continuations —
  translation twins (same content, mode A vs mode B). Metric: mean fp32
  per-token log-probability of each continuation under multi-depth injection.
  An episode's *mode margin* is llh(exposed-mode twin) − llh(other twin).
- **Generalization axis**: heldout **prompts** (and heldout demonstration sets),
  never-seen during training. Mode must transfer as a policy, not as replayed
  demonstrations.

## Corpus

Generated by frozen Qwen (`fable2/modes.py`): N≈54 prompt/twin-pair items
(30 train / 8 development / 16 heldout), plus disjoint demonstration pools per
split. Admission gates, behavioral as always:

1. Language identity of each twin verified by deterministic stopword/character
   heuristics (French twin identifies French, German twin German; no overlap).
2. Twin content parity: token-length ratio within [0.6, 1.67].
3. **Bare-model indifference**: |llh(A twin) − llh(B twin)| under the bare
   backbone below a preregistered band (0.5 nats/token) — the floor must not
   already prefer one mode, or margins measure the prior, not the organism.

## Treatment

Organism, graft, depths, optimizer, and all instruments identical to G2 (408
cells, seed 7, depths 0.25/0.5/0.75/1.0, lr 1e-3, 300 updates, eval every 25,
per-eval checkpoints). Objective: the G2 three-hinge structure over mode
margins — exposed-mode margin ≥ margin bound (differential, both arms live);
exposed-mode llh not below no-exposure (no-harm, detached baseline);
wrong-mode llh not below no-exposure (anti-sabotage). The loss is a sibling of
G2's `paired_contrast_loss` with the identical hinge structure, operating on
twin log-likelihoods instead of label log-softmaxes; both are contract-tested
against the sabotage configuration.

## Arms (same battery, mode semantics)

`normal` (exposed to matching-mode demos) · `wrong_passage` → **wrong-mode
demos** · `no_exposure` · `bare_floor` (organism-free) · `memory_lesion` ·
`internal_lesion` · per-depth lesions. Mode symmetry is enforced: every item is
evaluated once with A as the exposed mode and once with B; a policy that only
pushes toward one fixed language is a bias, not an acquired mode, and the
symmetric pairing cancels it.

## Pass gates (heldout, at the read point, all required)

1. Mean mode margin positive under both exposure directions, and `normal` beats
   `wrong-mode`, `no_exposure`, `bare_floor`, and both tissue lesions in mean
   correct-mode llh.
2. Strict wins ≥ majority (≥ 17/32 direction-episodes) vs wrong-mode exposure.
3. At least one depth lesion hurts a majority; tissue lesions cost > 0.01.
4. Anti-sabotage: wrong-mode arm not below no-exposure by more than 0.02.
5. **Replication rule (standing, from G2)**: a read-at-best pass counts only if
   it survives re-evaluation of that checkpoint on resampled demonstration sets
   and swapped mode assignments.

Train-time artifact (`fable2.curves`) required before interpretation;
read-at-best and the single sanctioned resume-to-600 on
`still_improving_at_stop` carry over unchanged.

## Stop rules

- Corpus generation failing its gates is an infrastructure result; do not weaken
  gates to fill quotas.
- A gate fail: stop. No scale/duration/data moves (all falsified in the G-line).
  A mode-line fail with healthy instruments would be the first evidence against
  the modulation-competence reading of the program's own record — that verdict
  would promote the H-line directly, with M-line diagnosis as a side quest.
- A pass promotes M1: delayed expression (mode retained across interleaved
  neutral traffic), then mode switching and reacquisition speed — the lifetime
  properties. H0 proceeds in parallel after the M0 verdict either way.

## Result

Complete 2026-08-07, 300 updates on the physical RTX 4090 (259 min, peak 14.9
GiB, zero gradient rejections, no late-run collapse). Artifacts:
`m0-acquired-language-mode-result.json`, `-trend.json`, `m0-corpus.json`,
`m0-u300-replication.json`, `figures/m0-*.png`.

**Verdict: qualified pass — the differential core replicated (the program's
first replicated positive result); the absolute per-direction symmetry clause
failed as written, for a measured instrument reason.**

Gates at the final update (no read-point selection; the final round passes
everything the best rounds pass):

1. Mean margins: wrong-mode +0.088 ✓, floor +1.302 ✓, no-exposure +0.036 ✓,
   memory lesion +0.036 ✓, internal lesion +0.037 ✓. **Per-direction clause
   fails**: French-exposed mode margin +0.185, German-exposed −0.013.
2. Strict wins vs wrong-mode: 30/32 ✓ (band 23–32/32 across all twelve rounds;
   never near the majority line).
3. Depth attribution: depth-15 lesion costs +0.285 (29/32), depth-7 +0.074
   (23/32); the two deep sites are mildly counterproductive ✓.
4. Anti-sabotage: wrong − no-exposure = +0.052 ✓ (wrong-mode demos still help
   over nothing; no sabotage optimum).
5. **Replication (the G2 rule): PASSED.** On fresh demonstration samples the
   differential held at +0.083 with 31/32 and 30/32 strict wins — numerically
   indistinguishable from the in-run values.

On the failed clause: the replication's per-direction decomposition shows a
standing French tilt in the injection stacked on the corpus floor's own +0.07
French tilt (admitted by a per-item band that never constrained the aggregate).
Exposure moves the margin by ~+0.11 (French) and ~+0.06 (German) *relative to
the floor* — conditional motion in both directions, with the German direction
starting 0.07 underwater and finishing at −0.013. This floor-anchored reading
is post-hoc and recorded as such; it does not amend this verdict. M1
preregisters floor-anchored margins as primary and an aggregate-tilt gate on
corpus admission, so the next assay cannot confuse a floor tilt with an
expression asymmetry.

What M0 established:

- **Mode acquisition through tissue is real.** Demonstrations experienced only
  by the organism, erased from every scoring context, shift frozen-Qwen
  behavior on never-seen prompts in the demonstrated direction — replicated,
  lesion-attributed (mid-depth injection sites and tissue both causal),
  sabotage-free by construction and by measurement.
- The modulation-competence reading of the program's record is confirmed: the
  same substrate, graft, objective, and instruments that failed three times on
  content transport passed on the first attempt at mode transport.
- `memory_lesion ≡ no_exposure` held again (margins identical to 4 decimals):
  acquisition still reads from the FIFO tape. Persistence beyond FIFO turnover
  is exactly M1's question.
- Train-time: mild oscillation with a small drift after u150; ~150 updates
  would likely have sufficed (recorded as overtraining-lite; nothing like the
  G-line toxicity).

Per the pass path: M1 (persistence) proceeds — M1a measures the retention
curve on this checkpoint (no training); M1b trains under delayed-expression
pressure. H0 remains queued in parallel.
